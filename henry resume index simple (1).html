Where I started college

Ocean Research College Academy

ORCA is the early college program at Everett Community College that I attended while earning my associate degree.

ORCA is a full-time Running Start program for high school juniors and seniors. Students take college classes together as a cohort, and the research is built into the coursework instead of sitting beside it.

The academy sits on the Everett waterfront at the Port of Everett. Students collect and analyze data on the health of Possession Sound, tracking water quality and the abundance and distribution of marine life. A research lab and a research vessel make that possible.

The program has a unique standing: EvCC describes it as the only program in the country where high school students conduct college-level original oceanography research. Students finish with an associate degree and a high school diploma, with college tuition covered through Running Start.

For me, ORCA meant learning to ask a question, gather evidence, and explain the result clearly. Those are the same habits I bring to marketing: test what works, trust the data, and communicate it well.
